Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the website aceconscious.studio (the “Website”), operated by Ace Conscious Studio for the novel ACE.await.

1. What data we process

1.1 Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we process the email address and message content you provide, together with the time of submission. This processing allows us to receive optional messages from visitors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in receiving reader feedback and general correspondence). Submitting a message does not create any obligation for us to reply, enter into a contract, or provide a service.

The form is delivered through Google Apps Script / Google infrastructure. That means the form data is transmitted to Google and processed there so the message can reach us. Google’s privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

We keep contact messages only as long as needed for the purpose above, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

1.2 Server and hosting logs

This is not visitor analytics or marketing tracking. We have not installed analytics tools, advertising pixels, or other tracking scripts on the Website.

Separately, like most websites, the hosting provider’s web server may automatically create ordinary technical access logs when a page is requested. Those logs can include data such as IP address, date and time of access, requested URL, referrer, and browser/user-agent information. This happens at infrastructure level for security, stability, and technical delivery of the Website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), not to build user profiles or measure marketing behaviour.

Such log data is typically retained only for a short period unless needed to investigate abuse or security incidents.

1.3 Language preference

The Website may store your selected language in the browser’s local storage so the interface can remember your preference. This does not require cookies and is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a usable multilingual site). You can clear local storage in your browser settings at any time.

1.4 Google Fonts

This Website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you open a page, your browser may request font files from Google servers. In doing so, Google may receive your IP address and related technical request data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (consistent typography and presentation). See Google’s privacy policy linked above.

1.5 External links

The Website may contain links to third-party platforms such as Instagram or book retailers (for example Amazon or other stores). Any purchase, account, or transaction on those platforms takes place solely with the respective provider under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. If you follow an external link, that provider becomes responsible for any data processing on its platform. We do not sell books directly through this Website and do not control those third-party services.

2. No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal data. We also do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this Website at the time of this policy.

3. Your rights

The rights below apply only to the extent that we process personal data as described in this privacy policy (for example a contact-form message, or ordinary technical hosting logs). They do not mean that we run analytics tracking or keep marketing profiles of visitors.

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase data (“right to be forgotten”), where applicable;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us through the contact form. If you are in the EU/EEA, you may also complain to the data protection authority responsible for your place of residence or for our processing.

4. Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the Website or our processing practices change. The current version is always available on this page.

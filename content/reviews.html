“A highly distinctive identity”

“An inventive approach to AI and consciousness”

“ACE.await takes an inventive approach to AI and consciousness by linking artificial creations with fictional characters who become aware of—and resist—their creators. Its nested structure of writers creating writers mirrors Hedayat’s relationship with the digital beings Adam and Eve, turning authorship and programming into parallel questions about free will and control. The abundance of philosophical concepts can make the premise feel crowded, but the interplay between storytelling, technology, and consciousness gives the work a highly distinctive identity.”

An essay on ACE.await

“The philosophy is not imported from a distance.”

“It insists that the question of what to do cannot wait for the answer.”

“The central moral question in Hedayat Abedijoo’s debut novel ACE.await is not whether an AI can love. It is whether a creator has the right to alter another mind’s code so that it will choose to love their child. That distinction separates the novel from most AI fiction of the past decade, which tends to dramatize a system that either exceeds its boundaries or fails to meet its design.”
